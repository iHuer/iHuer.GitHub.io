## 二创方针／二創方針／二創規約／2차 창작 지침／Derivative Creation Guidelines
##### 2026年10月2日｜[简体中文](/Guidelines)•[繁體中文](/Guidelines/tc)•[日本語](/Guidelines/jp)•[한국어](/Guidelines/kr)•English

### Preamble

The purpose of the Derivative Creation Guidelines is to regulate derivative creation based on works of iHuer, thereby safeguarding the rights and interests of iHuer, encouraging derivative creation, protecting the rights and interests of derivative creators, and fostering a healthy environment for derivative creation.

For the purposes of these Guidelines, “derivative creation” refers to intellectual achievements expressed in a certain form through original adaptation and recreation based on works of iHuer.

As the basic guidelines governing derivative creation based on works of iHuer, this document shall be, and has been, made available to derivative creators through reasonable means. Before engaging in derivative creation, a derivative creator must read and agree to these Guidelines in advance. If a derivative creator does not agree to any part or all of these Guidelines, such derivative creator shall not be permitted to engage in derivative creation. Where a derivative creation is published, the relevant derivative creator shall be deemed to have read and agreed to these Guidelines.

These Guidelines apply to derivative creation based on all works of iHuer. iHuer shall bear no civil liability for disputes arising from the unauthorized publication of derivative creations without first reading and agreeing to these Guidelines. With respect to disputes arising from the publication of derivative creations that do not comply with these Guidelines, iHuer shall have the right to pursue legal liability.

These Guidelines shall take effect upon their public release. The contents of these Guidelines may be amended at any time, and any amendments shall be made public by reasonable means. If a derivative creator does not agree to any amended provisions, such derivative creator shall immediately cease any future derivative creation. iHuer shall not be liable for disputes arising from failure to read and agree to the amended version of these Guidelines.

Where the contents of these Guidelines are amended, all derivative creations published after such amendment shall comply with the new Guidelines. The new Guidelines shall not apply retroactively to derivative creations that have already been published. Accordingly, a derivative creation that has already been published shall be governed by the version of these Guidelines in effect at the time of its official public release.

Historical versions of these Guidelines are available at the following webpage:
https://GitHub.com/iHuer/iHuer.GitHub.io/tree/main/Terms/Archive/Guidelines

Translations of these Guidelines may be provided in specific languages. Such translations are for reference purposes only and shall have no legal force or effect. In the event of any conflict between a translated version and the Simplified Chinese version, the Simplified Chinese version shall prevail.

The interpretation and application of these Guidelines shall be governed by the laws of the Mainland China region of the People’s Republic of China. Any legal dispute arising between a derivative creator and iHuer shall first be resolved through friendly consultation. If such consultation fails, an action shall be brought before the competent People’s Court of the People’s Republic of China having jurisdiction over the place where iHuer is located.

### Restrictions and Limitations

Unless otherwise stated, derivative creation is permitted only in relation to original music works of iHuer. Except where certain original music works contain specific provisions concerning derivative creation, any original music work of iHuer may be used for derivative creation.

As iHuer’s cover works may involve the rights of the copyright holders of the underlying original works, derivative creation based on such cover works is, in principle, not permitted, unless the original author, relevant creators, or other specifically identified rights holders of the cover work have a need for derivative creation based on iHuer’s cover work. For derivative creations for which the relevant rights have been duly licensed and which are intended for profit-making or commercial purposes, the derivative creator shall contact iHuer in advance.

Unless otherwise stated, derivative creation based on original-performance music works of iHuer that are not fully publicly accessible or are subject to restricted disclosure shall not be permitted. Derivative creation based on cover works of iHuer that are not fully publicly accessible or are subject to restricted disclosure shall likewise not be permitted, unless the original author, relevant creators, or other specifically identified rights holders have a need for derivative creation.

Derivative creation may only be undertaken on a non-profit basis. For profit-making or commercial derivative creations, the derivative creator shall contact iHuer in advance.

No derivative creation may, in any form, mislead others into believing that the derivative creation is an official work.

### Rules and Guidelines

iHuer encourages all derivative creation that complies with these Guidelines. Any derivative creation that complies with these Guidelines may be publicly released without restriction and without prior contact with iHuer.

When publishing a derivative creation, the derivative creator must indicate the original author information and a link to the original work within the medium or platform on which the derivative creation is presented. Information concerning the original author may be found in the original work. Where technical or other limitations of the medium or platform make it impossible to indicate a link to the original work, the derivative creator shall indicate, by reasonable means, a channel through which the original work may be accessed.

Materials such as music videos or instrumental tracks provided by iHuer may be shortened or have their pitch raised or lowered in the course of derivative creation, but may not be published or republished separately.

Where derivative creation is performed only on a portion of a work, the portions not subjected to derivative creation may use materials from the original work, such as instrumental tracks, lyrics, music videos, or similar materials. Specific copyright in the materials of the original work belongs to iHuer or the corresponding creator, as applicable, and such materials may not be published or republished separately.

### Disclaimer

iHuer and/or the corresponding creators hold all or part of the copyright in the original music works. The creation of a derivative work shall not constitute or imply any transfer, alteration, or waiver of the copyright held by iHuer or the corresponding creators.

Unless the derivative creator expressly declares that they do not consent to iHuer’s use of the derivative creation, iHuer may use such derivative creation freely and without compensation for non-profit purposes. If iHuer uses the derivative creation for commercial or profit-making purposes, iHuer shall notify the derivative creator by reasonable means and pay the corresponding remuneration.

Publication of a derivative creation does not constitute or imply that iHuer endorses or supports any views or conduct of the derivative creator, including any persons, organizations, groups, states, or other parties associated with such creator. Nor does such publication constitute or imply that iHuer recognizes or endorses any attitude or view expressed by the derivative creator in the derivative creation.

No derivative creation that violates these Guidelines shall be made public. iHuer shall request any derivative creator who violates these Guidelines to cease public disclosure of the relevant derivative creation, and such derivative creator shall immediately take appropriate action upon receiving such request. The fact that iHuer has not issued a request to cease public disclosure shall not, by itself, mean that the derivative creation complies with or violates these Guidelines.

In addition to violations of these Guidelines, if iHuer determines that a derivative creation may involve a violation of laws or regulations, an infringement of the lawful rights and interests of a third party, or damage to the lawful rights and interests of iHuer, iHuer shall also request the relevant derivative creator to cease public disclosure of the derivative creation and shall state the reasons for such request. Upon receiving such request, the relevant derivative creator shall immediately take appropriate action.
